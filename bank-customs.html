Manifest costing details

Included standard cost heads load automatically with blank actual amounts. Use Add Row and double-click its cost head for additional Port, C&F and Transport/Testing/Retirement costs. A head missing from the captured standard uses 0 BDT; variance equals actual minus standard. Every row requires a party selected by code and name. New standard costs use the item KG/LTR/PCS basis and multiply by converted manifest quantity. Historical captured pack-based standards retain their original basis. Enter total actual cost in BDT for this manifest.
